who I am

I'm Syed Abdur Rafay Shah, a self-taught motion graphic designer and animator based in Karachi, Pakistan. I build the visuals that introduce a brand, explain a product and carry a story in its first few seconds: brand films, SaaS explainers, 3D product animation, logo and title animation, and AI-assisted video.

what I focus on

I've spent three-plus years making motion and video for software and technology companies, including Fleekbiz, Sentra Core Systems and Art and Animation Studio. One of my trailers was shown on the big screens of Times Square, New York.

My toolkit is After Effects and Blender at the center, with Premiere Pro alongside, plus a generative AI stack (Higgsfield, Magnific and ElevenLabs) that I use only when it serves the shot better than the traditional route. I'm drawn to premium product visuals, technology brands, and projects where design and motion work together to make an idea feel clear, distinctive, and memorable.

What I make.

From frame-by-frame animation to AI-assisted production, I create visual content that helps brands communicate, showcase, and stand out.
